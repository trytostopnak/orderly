# Orderly pitch and demo

## Six-section pitch script

### 1. Problem
Independent bakers receive orders in chat messages and then rebuild the quantities, due dates and prep plan elsewhere. We are testing whether this repetitive step creates enough friction to justify a dedicated order desk. Customer discovery is still pending.

### 2. Solution
Orderly turns a pasted message into a reviewed order card and a combined kitchen preparation list, using the baker's menu and prices.

### 3. Product Demo
This is a fictional order from Ananya. I paste it into the Message desk. Orderly finds six choco chip cookies and four brownies. At the sample menu prices of INR 65 and INR 90, the items total INR 750. I check the date, time and pickup method, confirm the review, and save. The order appears in the order book and updates the kitchen list. A reply is drafted for me to review and copy. If a quantity is missing or a message changes an order, the tool highlights it for checking.

### 4. Traction
We have a working prototype and 14 passing automated checks of its order logic. We do not yet have verified customer interviews, signed pilots, revenue or measured customer time savings. We will measure time to a correct order, correction rate, and whether bakers return to use it the following week.

### 5. Build Journey
The first build narrowed the problem to a bakery's message-to-prep workflow. It implemented a local parser, manual review, order tracking and kitchen aggregation, and tested ambiguous quantities and dates. The next step is customer discovery and pilot feedback. We are not claiming a 20-day build history or completed 14-day GTM sprint.

### 6. Team
Add the actual builder and team names before submitting. Assign responsibility for customer discovery, implementation and pilot operations. Invite independent bakers to test five anonymized orders and give direct feedback.

## One-line pitch

Orderly helps home bakers turn messy customer messages into checked orders and a clear baking list. Our prototype is ready for customer testing.
